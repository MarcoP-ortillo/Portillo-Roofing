About Portillo Roofing

A family-owned business built on integrity, craftsmanship, and a commitment to raising the standard for roofing services in Central Texas.

Our Story

Portillo Roofing LLC was founded on a simple principle: deliver exceptional craftsmanship, honest pricing, and treat people with respect. After more than 25 years of subcontracting for large roofing companies, Marco Portillo Sr. recognized the need for a better approach— one built on integrity, transparency, and a genuine commitment to the communities we serve.

Through patience, perseverance, and a lifetime dedicated to the trade, Marco Sr. built a reputation founded on trust and quality workmanship. Those values became the cornerstone of Portillo Roofing LLC and continue to guide every project we undertake.

Today, I am proud to continue that legacy. Growing up around the roofing industry, I learned early that trust is earned through hard work, clear communication, and doing what is right even when no one is watching. Those principles carried me throughout my education and ultimately led me to earn my Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin.

By combining generations of hands-on roofing experience with engineering-driven problem solving, we strive to deliver solutions that are built to last and relationships that stand the test of time.

Marco Portillo Sr. and the Portillo Roofing team

Looking Toward the Future

While roofing remains our foundation, our vision extends far beyond installing shingles and metal panels.

We believe homes should be stronger, more efficient, and more sustainable. Our long-term mission is to explore innovative ways to improve how people live by rethinking the relationship between shelter, materials, energy, and the communities we build around them.

Guided by engineering principles and a deep respect for craftsmanship, we aspire to develop solutions that maximize the potential of existing materials, embrace sustainable building practices, and create homes designed to serve generations to come.

As Portillo Roofing continues to grow, our goal is to become more than a roofing company. We seek to be a trusted partner in building resilient homes, strengthening communities, and helping shape a more sustainable future.
